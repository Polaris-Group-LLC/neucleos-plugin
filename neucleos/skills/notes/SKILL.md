---
name: notes
description: "Notes and action items from a recording of a call or a meeting, in Neucleos: transcribed (priced first, by the minute), then a canvas of what was decided and an Action items dataset with owners and dates."
---

Make notes and action items from the recording the person named with this command. If they did not say which, ask in one short question first, or offer to add the file.

1. Read the guide: guide with the topics words, recordings, canvases and datasets.
2. world_list, then find the recording (search, artifact_list with kind media). If it is a file on their computer, file_upload gives an address: put it there with your shell, then media_add takes it in.
3. media_transcript says what transcribing it costs at most: say that price, and with their yes, media_transcribe. Then read it all with media_transcript, part by part.
4. Ask who the speakers are if it matters and they have not said. Make a canvas of the decisions, what was discussed and the open questions, with the recording on it, and keep the action items in an Action items dataset (task, owner, due date, done, when it came up) shown live on the canvas. Add to an existing Action items dataset for the same work rather than make another.
5. artifact_open puts the canvas in front of them; if their cockpit is not open, cockpit_link gives a link that opens it signed in (open it or give it to them as its description says, and tell them where it opened).
6. Tell them in a few lines what was decided and who owes what by when. It is private to them: share it only if they ask.
