---
name: briefing
description: "Write a briefing in Neucleos as a canvas: a page to read, with text, pictures, live datasets and components, made from what the person gives or from their datasets."
---

Write the briefing the person asked for with this command as a canvas in Neucleos. If they did not say what it is about, ask in one short question first.

1. Read the guide: guide with the topics words and canvases.
2. world_list, then search for what it draws on: a dataset, earlier research, files they mention.
3. canvas_create, then canvas_write: a title, the summary first, a section for each point, @@dataset blocks for the facts that should stay live, and pictures from files they give (file_upload, then media_add).
4. artifact_open puts it in front of them; if their cockpit is not open, cockpit_link gives a link that opens it signed in (open it or give it to them as its description says, and tell them where it opened).
5. Tell them what it covers. It is private to them: share it only if they ask.
