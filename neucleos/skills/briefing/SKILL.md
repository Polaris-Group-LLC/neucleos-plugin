name: briefing
description: "Write a briefing in Neucleos as a canvas: a page to read, with text, pictures, live datasets and components, made from what the person gives or from their datasets."
---

Write the briefing the person asked for with this command as a canvas in Neucleos. If they did not say what it is about, ask in one short question first.

1. Read the guide with topics words and canvases, and clients when the brief concerns a client, account, prospect, deal or discovery work.
2. world_list, then find the existing brief and work before creating a duplicate. For discovery, work_resume, claim_find and source_search within the work; read the cited units and keep any new findings as claims. Preserve the person's own notes and state unread material and unresolved questions.
3. Use canvas_read for an existing page, otherwise canvas_create. canvas_write puts the summary first, then a section for each point, @@dataset blocks for live facts and source images where useful. Discovery statements cite claims as [^c12]. If the page is stale, reconcile its evidence and pass rests.refreshToken from canvas_read to canvas_write; an unrelated edit does not clear warnings. Sharing must also allow access to the supporting work and sources. Make a separate sanitized page for a wider audience.
4. artifact_open puts it in front of them; if their cockpit is not open, cockpit_link gives a link that opens it signed in (open it or give it to them as its description says, and tell them where it opened).
5. Tell them what it covers. It is private to them: share it only if they ask.
