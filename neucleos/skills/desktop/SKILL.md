name: desktop
description: "Start a Neucleos desktop for what the person is working on: things they can use at once (notes, lists, a table, a poll, a form) beside the chat, opened in their cockpit."
---

Make the person a desktop in Neucleos for what they asked for with this command. If they did not say what it is for, ask in one short question first.

1. Read the guide: guide with the topics words and desktops.
2. world_list, and work in their home World unless they named another. Look before you make (search, artifact_list with kind desktop): reopen a desktop that already fits rather than make a near-duplicate.
3. desktop_create, then desktop_write: a few things they can use at once, such as a note of what it is for, a list of next steps and a table of what matters, not prose alone.
4. artifact_open puts it in front of them. If their cockpit is not open, cockpit_link gives a link that opens it signed in: open it in your browser if you have one, or give it to them, saying it works once, within a minute.
5. Tell them in two lines what is on it and what you could add next.
