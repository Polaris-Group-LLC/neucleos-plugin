# brands

A brand is how a World looks and writes: its colors, type, shapes, logos, the patterns its pages repeat and its voice, each entry saying how sure it is and where it came from. Look first (search, artifact_list): extend the World's brand rather than make a second. Make or change one with brand_create, brand_read and brand_write, in its one format. Outside blocks, the brand book: a "# Name" line, a "themes:" line ("themes: day (light), night (dark)"; the first theme is the one a value falls back to), an optional "fonts:" line in words, then a paragraph or three of what the brand is. Then one block per entry: "@@color tomato", "@@type display", "@@space step-4", "@@radius", "@@shadow", "@@gradient", "@@motion", "@@layout", "@@pattern Price tag", "@@voice Stance", "@@imagery Photography", "@@logo Stamp", "@@surface Website", "@@rule", each closed by "@@end". Inside, "key: value" lines (several may share a line, joined by " · "): a value per theme where it differs ("day: #c8341f", "night: #ff6a4d"; or "value:"), "role:" where the entry fills one of the roles every World has (text, muted, faint, ground, surface, surface-2, line, line-2, accent, on-accent, good, warn, bad, focus, radius, font, display, mono; each once), "usage:", "avoid:", "on:", "sure:" (exact: read from a source file; measured: computed from pictures of pages; estimated: judged by eye; proposed: made up for what the sources never had; per theme when they differ, "sure: night exact · day proposed"), "from:" (the pages or files it came from), and for a type style "font:" (family and weight), "size:", "tracking:", "case:", "sample:"; for a logo "media:" (its file's name in the World's Files: add it with media_add first, copied from the sources, never drawn), "ink:", "min:", "clear:"; for a pattern "component:" (the widget that draws it). A color is #hex, rgb(), oklch() or another color by name in braces ("{violet}"). Color, type, space, radius, shadow, gradient, motion and layout names are lowercase letters, digits and hyphens; the rest are plain words. A pattern, voice, imagery, surface or rule says what it is in its body, in Markdown: a pattern's anatomy, when to use it and when not, and the pages it came from; a voice from the founder's own words, quoted, with what to avoid, and a register per surface. Never guess a value and mark it exact. brand_read lists contrast under 4.5:1 per theme (3:1 for lines that carry meaning and focus rings): keep a source's own colors and say so; propose an added theme rather than retint. A brand needs text, ground and accent roles before it can be released. Fonts: file_upload with for: font and the brand, then brand_font_add with its licence in words; only a licence that allows the web is served on public pages. People change values and approve entries in its tab; brand_read names what they changed. Releasing a brand and things wearing it come next in Neucleos; until then, when you make something for a World with a brand, read the brand first and use its colors, type and voice.
