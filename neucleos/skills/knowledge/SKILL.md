# knowledge

Knowledge is a capability of every enabled World, with the same tools and access rules. Start with world_list to choose the intended World, then world_map to build or refresh its free counted index. knowledge_status reports coverage, pending summaries, the current job and spending settings. knowledge_graph gives declared and reviewed relationships plus unresolved evidence and stale dependents, and a workspace URL for the Knowledge screen. Follow nextOffset until null; disclose any partial coverage. A source in another World is a gap, not permission to move or share it.
Declared links come from references, page citations, source names with exact locators, client records and work evidence. They reflect additions, edits, removals and access changes immediately. related follows a thing or exact row, claim or source ordinal one or two steps: direction out for its evidence, in for what depends on it. Read the actual row or source passage before making a factual claim. A common name or topic does not establish a dependency.
Written summaries and suggested row relationships use the World's credits. knowledge_refresh without go estimates the pending work; with the person's authorization, its founder or admin sets monthlyCeiling in dollars and keepCurrent. Pass the estimate's ceilingMicros with go to start; knowledge_status reports progress and charges. stop ends the current job and releases its unused hold. Starting again resumes only pending work. Unchanged content is not charged again. With keepCurrent enabled, the job updates changed material after ten quiet minutes, with a nightly sweep as a fallback. Turning keepCurrent off pauses future automatic writing; it does not by itself stop a running job.
Inferred relationships stay proposed, with exact quotations from accessible locations. link_write saves a proposal; link_review confirms or rejects it when asked. Contradictions require evidence from both sides, with dates and scenarios distinguished; never choose a winner merely because a model proposed a link. Rejected suggestions are preserved. A confirmed relationship becomes stale when its evidence changes; review the current passages before updating it.
The Knowledge screen has a map, a list of things, their summaries and parts, and Review with proposed links and changed evidence. Opening a node or an evidence link opens its actual page, row or source passage. To reconcile a dependent page, read it and the changed evidence, use canvas_write to make the warranted edits while preserving the person's own work, then knowledge_reconcile to record the review. Refreshing the index does not clear these warnings or silently rewrite a person's page.
