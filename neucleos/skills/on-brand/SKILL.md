The person has requested Neucleos work. Follow the opening skill first if the workspace is not already open in this conversation.

Make what the person asked for in their World's brand and voice.

1. guide with the topic brands; brand_read the brand it will wear (the World's, or the one they named).
2. Write in the brand's register for where it will be seen, with its patterns and none of its avoided words.
3. Style only with the roles (var(--n-text), var(--n-accent)…) and the brand's own colors (var(--b-<name>, var(--n-<role>))), never literal colors: a page, a widget or an app then wears the brand by itself. Use its patterns' widgets (component_search) before writing new ones.
4. For a PowerPoint, Word or PDF file made elsewhere, brand_files gives the fonts, the Office theme and the logos.
5. Say which brand and theme it wears; brand_use changes either.
