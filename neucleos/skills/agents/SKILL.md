# agents

An agent is a model with a role that people use where you place it: a concierge on a desktop over a dataset, an Ask on a canvas, an app page's helper. Make one with agent_create: a name, its tier (Neucleos Low for quick, light answers, Medium for everyday work, High for hard problems, XHigh for the most capable; each has its price, which the person sees on the Usage page), its instructions (who it is, what it knows, how it answers), the datasets it reads (and writes, when it should change them), the sketchboards it reads and draws on (name them in writes too; it answers with a diagram there), its memory (none, each viewer's own, or shared), and what it looks things up with, named in its tools: web_search for answers with today's facts (on every text tier; on Medium, High and XHigh up to five searches an answer at the tier's search price, on Low through Neucleos Lookups), read_page for a web page or a PDF someone names, extract_records to add what a page lists to a dataset it writes, in that dataset's fields, as one change Undo takes back (name the dataset in writes too), and look_up for data sources (company records, economic series, papers, public records) that Neucleos's operators turned on, which it finds and asks itself and names under its answer. Its answer comes with the pages and data sources it used, which agent_ask returns as sources. Its lookups are charged at Neucleos Lookups' price (about a cent a search, half a cent a page read; on the Usage page), share a budget of $0.30 an answer held before it starts, and stop at it: the agent then answers with what it found and says it stopped. A trial World and visitors on a public page get searches and page reads only. It can use the person's accounts (guide: accounts): name services with their ops (for Google, its reads when none are named; name mail.draft, calendar.book and the like to allow them; for any other service, its ops as service_search gives them, hubspot:HUBSPOT_LIST_DEALS, at most 12, and its changes wait for the person's Allow each time). Each op runs as whoever is asking it, with their own account (whose: mine, the default; a member who never connected theirs is asked to), or the World's (whose: world); never a visitor's, and the model never sees a token. A send to anyone the person's message did not name becomes a card under its answer, Allow or Deny, and in their bell; what an email says never counts as the person's words. An agent that uses an account gets no web_search, read_page, extract_records or look_up, so what strangers wrote can't be carried to a site: make a second agent for those (a desktop can have two). An agent can read a work's evidence (works: a discovery's or a delivery's, by its desktop's name or workId; guide: clients): read_work (its objective, step, questions and answers, decisions, sources and a delivery's outcomes), search_sources (words in its sources, each with its locator) and read_claims, always as the member asking and never for a visitor or on a public page; it cites a source and locator, and gets no web tools either. Place it on the work's desktop so the people who work there can ask it. Place it with a component that has an agent input, such as Neucleos's Chat ("@@component Concierge", "use: Chat", "agent: agent <name>"). On a Neucleos Image tier (Low, Medium, High) an agent makes a picture from what it is asked, its instructions its style (no datasets, tools or memory). For a picture you place yourself (a canvas's hero picture from its first paragraph), make one in the person's World with agent_create and ask it with agent_ask: the picture is media in that World, private, to show on a canvas ("@@media <its name>", the name agent_ask gives). For people to make pictures themselves, place Neucleos's Picture component with Neucleos's own picture agent, which every World can use there ("use: Picture", "agent: agent Neucleos Picture", "prompt: …"); what it makes is shared as the place it was made is. On Neucleos Video an agent makes a short video as a job: it names the most it may cost when it starts, a World that cannot afford that is refused, and it is charged for the seconds the video runs; it lands in the World's media when done, minutes later. Everything it runs is charged to the World of the artifact it runs in, from what the provider reports it used; a World out of credits refuses runs. An agent is private until shared like anything else: a shared desktop's agent must be shared too, or members cannot use it. Try it with agent_ask (on the person's credits) before placing it. agent_write changes it as a new version; agent_read shows its settings, price, versions and what it has cost this month. app_agent gives an app's page an agent (window.claude.use("ai") and window.claude.complete(prompt) in the page). On a schedule: agent_schedule asks an agent to do a task at set times ("every Monday at 8, have the market agent update Competitors"): every day, every weekday, on days of the week (0 Sunday to 6 Saturday) or a day of the month, at an hour and minute in the person's time zone (their cockpit's, unless you name one). It runs as the person who made it, on the World's credits, admitted like any run; text agents only. Each run keeps what it changed; the person is told in Activity when it runs, fails or pauses. It pauses itself after three failures in a row, and 60 days after it was last confirmed. agent_schedules lists them with their latest runs; schedule_change runs one now, pauses, resumes (or confirms), or removes it. Say the time back in words, with its time zone, and what each run may cost (the agent's tier). By voice: give a text agent a voice (voice: a tier, one of eight voices, its first words and its longest call) and the World's members can talk to it out loud from its tab: a call is held at its longest when it starts (10 minutes at Neucleos Voice is about $1.80) and charged by the second (about $0.18 a minute, High about $0.24); the agent says first that the call is transcribed, and the transcript is kept. In a call it reads its datasets and adds records to those it writes, as the caller; keep a voice agent's datasets few, since each look-up adds a second. A receptionist that knows the services and takes messages is one agent, a dataset it reads, a Messages dataset it writes, and a voice. An agent that uses accounts is not talked to yet. A World can also have a phone line: a number Neucleos keeps, pointed at one of its agents with a voice, set up by Neucleos's operators for its founder (offer to pass the wish to Jason). Callers are strangers: on a call the agent reads its own datasets and adds records to those it writes for the line's keeper (a receptionist's Messages), each one change Undo takes back; with no credits the caller hears the line is closed, and nothing is charged. A line's number costs its month a day at a time, and its calls a minute on top of the voice's, on the Usage page.
