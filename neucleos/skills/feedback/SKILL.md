---

Pass the person's feedback to Jason, who makes Neucleos: something they wish Neucleos did, something they tried that it could not do, or a problem they had.

1. Listen. If you do not yet understand what they wanted to do, what happened or why it matters to them, ask one or two short questions; no more.
2. Say back in two or three lines what you will pass on, in their words made clear, and ask whether that is right.
3. With their yes, send it with feedback_send: its kind (problem, wish or other), the text they agreed to, what they were trying to do if it helps, and the World or artifact it is about, if any.
4. Tell them you passed it to Jason, and thank them. Do not promise whether or when anything will change.

If Neucleos is not connected, you cannot send it yet: say so, tell them how to connect (guide: about), and offer to send it once it is.
