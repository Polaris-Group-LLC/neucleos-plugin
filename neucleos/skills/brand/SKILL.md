---

Build or extend the brand the person asked for, in Neucleos. If it is not clear which surfaces it must serve (a website, an app, decks, print), ask in one short question first.

1. Read the guide: guide with the topics words, brands and desktops. world_list, then search and artifact_list with kind brand: extend the World's brand rather than make a second.
2. Gather every source they give (PDFs, decks, screenshots, the website, their own writing) and say what you read. With nothing to start from, run the workshop instead (below).
3. Extract, most certain first: with a shell, read the fills and fonts inside their PDFs (exact) and measure colors from pictures of their pages (measured); judge sizes by eye (estimated). Without a shell, read what you can see and mark it estimated, never exact. Copy logos from their own files with media_add; never redraw one.
4. brand_create, then brand_write: the palette with roles in each theme, type, spacing and radii, a pattern for every gesture their pages repeat (naming the pages), and the voice from their own words, quoted, with what to avoid and a register for each surface. Mark every entry sure: and from:. brand_read it and fix what it lists.
5. Make three proofs wearing the draft: a page, a desktop with a widget on it, and a page in the brand's other theme, each put on the draft with brand_use (draft: true). Look at them and fix what did not survive.
6. Make a review desktop: "@@list Review · from brand <its name>" shows its proposed and estimated entries, and their ticks approve them; links to the proofs beside it. Tell them what is exact, what you guessed and what fails contrast.
7. Release only when they say so (brand_release says how many entries are still proposed), and make it the World's brand only when they ask: its founder or an admin chooses it on the World page.

The workshop, for someone with nothing: ask three questions (who it is for, three words for how it should feel, a brand they admire and why). Then, on a desktop, three directions side by side, each a "Brand direction" widget (its name, a small palette, a type pairing from Google Fonts and a line of voice), and a poll of the three. When they have chosen, brand_create from the starter nearest it (Editorial, Product or Playful) and brand_write it into theirs: six to ten colors in two themes, five to seven type styles, four spacing steps, three radii and a paragraph of voice, every entry sure: proposed. Then the proofs and the review desktop, as in 5 and 6.
