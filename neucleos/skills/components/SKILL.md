# components

Components are compiled in development and registered for reuse. Discover existing pieces with object_catalog and component_search; configure their data in production. Use object_catalog to discover the reusable pieces installed in this environment. Use object_list with its continuation cursor to find saved work, then object_read before changing it. New workspace Pages, Desktops and Sites use object_create with their config, placements and bindings. Store ordinary text in the Page or Desktop itself: a placement has blockId, content:{kind:"text",markdown:"..."}, and layout. Give each owned block a stable ID, unique within its parent. Reference independently saved items with childId, selection and layout. Keep a local Table in its parent too: content:{kind:"table",sourceId,selection:{mode:"live"},columns:[...],limit:20}. Draw a Chart the same way: content:{kind:"chart",sourceId,selection:{mode:"live"},chart:"line"|"area"|"bar"|"donut"|"funnel"|"scatter"|"heatmap",x:"<field key>",y:["<number field key>"],aggregate:"none"|"sum"|"average"|"count",limit:100}. Give every Chart block a title. A count takes y:[] and a donut or funnel takes one y field. A scatter takes a number x, one y, aggregate:"none" and optional label:"<text field key>". A heatmap takes by:"<field key>" for its rows, an aggregate and at most one y field. Show dated records as a Timeline: content:{kind:"timeline",sourceId,selection:{mode:"live"},view:"story"|"gantt",title:"<field key>",start:"<date field key>",limit:50} with optional end and description; a gantt view also takes group, status and scale, and a story view takes image and layout. Give every Timeline block a title. A calculator can also use figures from a Dataset: content:{kind:"calculator",sourceId,selection:{mode:"live"},calculator:{inputs:[...],results:[...]},values:[{id:"total_cost",field:"<number field key>",aggregate:"sum"}]}, with a title; its formulas use each value by id. Put dated records on a Calendar the same way: content:{kind:"calendar",sourceId,selection:{mode:"live"},title:"<field key>",start:"<date field key>",limit:50} with optional end, group, description and view:"month"|"week"|"agenda". Give every Calendar block a title. Show figures the same way: content:{kind:"metrics",sourceId,selection:{mode:"live"},presentation:"grid"|"strip"|"rows"|"list"|"gauge",items:[{label:"Revenue",field:"<number field key>",aggregate:"sum"|"average"|"count"|"min"|"max"|"latest",trendBy:"<date field key>",format:"currency",currency:"USD"}]}, with a title on the block. A Site has its own header and footer as settings, config.header and config.footer, never as blocks on a Page; object_catalog lists them as site:header and site:footer, and object_catalog with block "site-header" or "site-footer" gives every setting. A Form asks text, long, number, date, email, yes, choice, rating (optional max), scale (optional min, max, minLabel, maxLabel) and matrix (rows and options) questions; set presentation:"signup" with one email question for an email signup. Page sections are display blocks: object_catalog lists them as block:<key> with an example, and content:{kind:"block",block:"hero",version:1,props:{...}} places one. Use a picture from the World's Media by setting its src to /_media/<artifactId>. sourceId identifies the saved Dataset object; each Table keeps its own settings and the rows remain shared. Keep Media, Design, Whiteboard and source-reader views local too: content contains kind and the original mediaId, designId, sketchboardId or sourceId. Use object_place_media to append a Media or Design view. Design selection is live or pinned to its original revision number. Whiteboard drawing elements remain on the original; public Site publication of a Whiteboard is not supported. Kinds marked placementOnly in the catalog cannot be created as independent objects. Do not create hidden Text, Table or view objects. Every Poll option, Quiz question and option, Form field and choice, Board column and card, and List item has an id you supply, unique in its scope (letters, digits, _ or -). Keep each id when you edit: labels can change and answers stay attached. A Quiz answer names its option id. Once a Poll, Quiz or Form has answers, renames and additions are allowed, but removing or rewording what people answered is refused. Live references follow current content; historical selections require editor access. Published Sites keep their own frozen content. object_update requires the revision read before editing. Editors use object_history to find older parent or block snapshots, then object_read with a revisionId to inspect them. Use object_open to leave the result saved and usable. Each surface uses the World's brand. Never compile UI code in production; if a needed piece is missing, identify that gap. Shared Sites publish through site_preview and site_publish with the returned buildId; site_preview attaches a hostname and captures saved content. site_publication reads versions and site_withdraw takes the website offline. Legacy websites retain their existing workflow until migrated. Existing legacy content remains readable and editable through its original tools; object_migration_plan reports which saved content can be migrated without loss.
