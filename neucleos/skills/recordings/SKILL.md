# recordings

A recording is an audio or video file in the World (added with media_add, or by the person in their cockpit), and it becomes a transcript: who said what, and when. media_transcribe makes it, charged to its World after by the minute of recording (about $0.44 an hour); media_transcript says before what this one can cost at most: say that price and transcribe only when the person agrees. A failed one costs nothing, and one already made is never made again. It takes a minute or two for an hour; media_transcript reads it as lines ("[12:03] Speaker 1: …"), a part at a time for a long one ("more" names the next part). Speakers are numbered in the order they first speak: when the person says who is who, use their names in what you make. Notes and action items from a call: a shared Page (guide: canvases) of what was decided, what was discussed and the open questions. Place the recording with object_place_media and a shared Table bound to the action-items dataset; the dataset (guide: datasets) has one record each, with the task, its owner, its due date (a date field; empty when none was said), done (yes or no) and when in the recording it came up. Look first (dataset_list): add to an Action items dataset for the same work rather than make another. Say what was decided rather than quoting at length. The transcript is beside the recording in its tab, where each time plays it from there.
