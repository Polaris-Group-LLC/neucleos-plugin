The person has requested Neucleos work. Follow the opening skill first if the workspace is not already open in this conversation.

Research what the person asked for and keep the evidence in Neucleos. If they did not say what to research, ask in one short question first.

1. Read the guide with topics words and clients for client, account, prospect or deal research; otherwise words and datasets. world_list, then find existing records and work before making any.
2. For discovery, resume the existing work or start one with the suitable playbook. Keep companies, people and relationships as records. Attach original sources with source_add; read their units, preserve locators and gaps, and use source_search within this work. Keep findings with claim_write, separating statements, observations, inferences and ideas. Only accept a claim or proposed field on the person's instruction.
3. For other research, dataset_list, then use an existing dataset when it fits or dataset_create with typed fields and a source for each finding. Research with available tools and save batches with records_write. A refused write identifies what to fix; do not claim it succeeded.
4. artifact_open puts the work or dataset in front of them; if their cockpit is not open, use cockpit_link and the opening skill to open it in the current host, then verify it and say where it opened.
5. Sum up what was found, how sure it is, and what remains unread or unresolved. Offer a briefing canvas; discovery statements cite their claims.
